About the course

      
CSC418 introduces students of the Department of Computer Science at COMSATS University Islamabad, Abbottabad Campus, to the culture, practices and tools that unite development and operations.

      
Through weekly labs — including deploying a static website on an Azure Ubuntu VM — students gain practical, industry-relevant cloud skills.
